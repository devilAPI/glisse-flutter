## 0.1.0

* First release: a Flutter port of Glisse's Liquid Glass.
* `Glass`, `GlassButton`, `GlassSwitch`, `GlassSlider`, `GlassSegmentedControl`, `GlassTabBar`, `GlassMenu`, `GlassInput`, `GlassSearchField`.
* One fragment shader for the lens: bezel refraction, dome lenses, chromatic dispersion, frost, rim light.
* `GlassBackdrop` for refraction on every renderer; Impeller's live backdrop where available; frost elsewhere.
